I’m looking for PhD opportunities in quantum computing and theoretical computer science, with a particular interest in quantum learning theory.

I teach in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at BRAC University in Dhaka, where I’m also pursuing my MSc. My research asks how many copies of an unknown quantum state we need to reconstruct it when we measure each qubit separately.

I completed my BSc at BRAC University in 2023. I’ve also written machine learning lessons for JetBrains Academy.

Quantum research

  • Learning Sparse Pure States with Single-Qubit Pauli Measurements (PDF)
    MSc thesis in progress. I use single-qubit Pauli measurements to find the basis states with nonzero amplitudes and recover their relative phases. A two-qubit example.
  • Adaptive Learning of Forest Graph States (PDF)
    MSc Network Science course project. I studied whether choosing each measurement using earlier outcomes can reduce the number of copies needed to recover the edges of an unknown forest.
